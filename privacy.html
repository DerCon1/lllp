Privacy

Privacy notice

We collect less because we need less.

This notice explains what Long Live Libre Press collects, why we collect it, and what happens to it. The short version: if you join the Reading List, we use your email address to send you the things you asked for.

Who we are

Long Live Libre Press is operated by Derek Connor. For privacy questions, requests or concerns, contact hello@longlivelibrepress.com.

What we collect

If you join the Reading List, we collect your email address and information needed to administer that subscription.

Our mailing-list provider may also record subscription events such as when you joined, confirmed, unsubscribed, or interacted with an email, where those features are enabled.

If you email us directly, we will also receive whatever information you choose to include in that message.

Why we use it

We use Reading List data to send occasional letters, new collections, essays and related updates from Long Live Libre Press. The legal basis for this is your consent.

We use information sent directly to us to answer enquiries and deal with the matter you contacted us about.

Double opt-in

Joining the Reading List requires confirmation by email. Until you confirm, you are not added as an active subscriber. You can withdraw your consent at any time by using the unsubscribe link in an email or by contacting us.

Who processes the data

We use a small number of service providers to run Long Live Libre Press:

  • Kit — signup forms, subscriber records and Reading List emails.
  • Google Workspace — business email and administrative communication.
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting.
  • Google Fonts — web fonts used by the site are currently loaded from Google's servers.

These providers may process technical data needed to provide their services. Some processing may take place outside the EEA or UK using recognised safeguards or transfer mechanisms where required.

How long we keep it

We keep your Reading List information while you remain subscribed. If you unsubscribe, we may keep a minimal suppression record so that we can respect that choice and avoid re-adding you accidentally.

Direct correspondence is kept only for as long as it is reasonably useful or necessary for the matter concerned.

Cookies and analytics

We do not currently run our own advertising system or behavioural analytics on this site. The embedded Reading List form and external services used to deliver the site may still make technical requests or use necessary service data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent, and to receive a copy of data you have provided.

To exercise any of these rights, email hello@longlivelibrepress.com. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as Long Live Libre Press grows or as the services we use change. The current version will always be published here.
