Books are better when they lead somewhere.

Long Live Libre Press is an independent reading project built around discovery, curation and the connections between books.

We are creating carefully assembled reading collections, original editorial work and new ways to help people find what to read, why it matters and where it might lead them next.

Because the value of a good book rarely ends on its final page.
