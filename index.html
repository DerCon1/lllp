For a reading life.

Long Live Libre Press is an independent project devoted to books, discovery and the life that develops around reading.

We believe good books deserve to remain in circulation — passed between people, rediscovered, argued with, written in, returned to and connected with other ideas.

We are building thoughtful curation, original editorial work and reading experiences designed to help people discover what to read, why it matters and where it might lead them next.
